# Northern Peaks Kitchen — Grab & Go Digital Menu & QR Sign

Mobile-friendly **Grab & Go Digital Menu** and print-ready **Scannable QR Counter Sign** for **Northern Peaks Kitchen** (Shop 2, 9 Westralia St, Stuart Park, Darwin, NT).

---

## ⚡ What Customers See When Scanning the QR Code

Scanning the QR code opens **[`index.html`](index.html)** (`https://prashantrijal40.github.io/NorthernPeaks_GrabAndGO/`):

1. **⚡ Grab & Go Menu (Default Tab)**
   * **$14.50 Morning Brekky Combo:** 1 Gourmet Bite + 1 Regular 8oz Barista Coffee.
   * **Hot Toasties & Brioche Rolls (5):** Bacon & Egg Roll, Ham & Cheese Toastie / Croissant, Chicken & Avocado Toastie, Cheese & Tomato Sourdough (VEG), Mushroom, Cheese & Tomato (VEG).
   * **Italian Bakery & Pastries (3):** Cornetto Mandorla (Almond), Cornetto Cioccolato, Artisan Cinnamon Scroll.
   * **Included 8oz Barista Drinks & Upgrades:** Flat White, Cappuccino, Latte, Long Black, Espresso, Piccolo, Macchiato, Mocha, Spiced Chai, Hot Chocolate + $1.00 upgrades (12oz Mug, Alt Milks, Extra Shot, Decaf, Syrups).
   * **Clear Counter Ordering Instructions:** Order & pay at the counter (EFTPOS, Card, Apple Pay, Cash) + tap-to-call (`0424 203 924`) & Google Maps directions.
2. **☕ Standalone Drinks Menu Tab** — Full coffee, iced coffee/matcha, frappes, smoothies, milkshakes, blooming teas & cold-pressed juices.
3. **🍽️ All-Day Dine-In Menu Tab** — Full dine-in breakfast, sweet starts, salads, poke bowls, burgers & kids menu while `northernpeaks.com.au` is being finalised.
